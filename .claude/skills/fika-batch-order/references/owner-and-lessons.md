# What the owner wants, and what went wrong the first time (batch 3, Nov 2026)

Read this before building or presenting an order. Each point cost a round of corrections.

## How the owner works

- **Kilograms only** when talking to them. Bags/packs only in the ERT Excel. Even notes in the owner's view must not
  say "6 bags"; say "about 16 kg".
- They want to **see** the sheet in the chat: send the HTML with display "render" (side panel) and the Excel as an
  attachment. After every change, resend both.
- They ask "what are you basing this on?" often. Have the answer ready, split into three groups: measured from data,
  taken from their sheets / their words, and your own estimates (with the effect if each estimate is wrong).
- They decide; you recommend. When their number disagrees with the data (Pomegranate Ovals 30 kg vs "1.5 kg a day"),
  show the conflict in one table and ask; do not silently pick one.
- When they set an amount, keep it fixed and re-split the rest. When they add a new candy inside the same batch
  size, take its kilograms from the candies with the most time left.
- They review hard. Before saying "final", run the review checklist and admit errors found (a mistake found by the
  owner later costs more trust than one you report yourself).

## Corrections the owner made (do it this way from the start)

1. First drafts were based only on website loose sales and used a 30-day window. Owner: use **everything since the
   batch arrived** (28 Sep), and see **how many of each candy** were ordered.
2. Owner suggested ignoring the Ready Mixes "as they hold every candy equally". Correct analysis: ignoring them makes
   slow sellers run out a month early, because the mixes take the same kilograms from every candy. Explain this,
   then **include mix use** (the owner agreed).
3. The goal is **one common run-out day for all candies**, not "proportional to sales". Proportional splits with
   per-tier safety days and round-up-to-bag left run-out dates from day 49 to day 128.
4. **Every candy** must be in the batch (minimum one pack), including returning ones (Fizzy Pop).
5. Candies with no sales this batch: the owner pointed to the earlier batch's data (August), not guesses. Use the
   share of loose sales when they were last on sale, and mark the rows as estimates.
6. Pomegranate Ovals: the August share (14.8%, launch week, sold out in 8 days) gave about 100 kg. Owner settled on
   80 kg after seeing the realistic range (60-70 kg if it sells like the best seller). Show such ranges.
7. Sour Strawberry Vanilla Diamonds appear on the FIKA Orders "Order 2" tab but were **never bought**. Always ask
   which lines of a supplier sheet were actually delivered.
8. The supplier sends some BUBS candies as **small retail packs** (175 g x 12, 190 g x 14, 155 g x 12). Use the pack
   the supplier actually sells (latest real order), not the 2.6-3.2 kg bulk sizes.

## Mistakes caught in my own review

- Ready Mix bags from the old site (no "Amount" label, $12.50) were counted as 100 g instead of 500 g; the Sour Mix
  looked like 16 kg instead of 22 kg. Always check mix kilograms = bags x 500 g.
- Raspberry Bites comes in 4 kg packs (ERT ARO7301), not 3 kg.
- Sour Pineapple's price on the ERT ORDER tab (17.68 SEK per 3 kg) is wrong; Order 1 says 193.36 SEK.
- The FIKA Orders cost plan for batch 3 ($14.39/kg) leaves VAT blank; add about $840.
- Fine-tuning to the batch weight first added a pack to Loose Teeth, which pushed it from day 49 to day 80. Only add
  a pack where the candy stays within a week of the common day.
- The last 7 days of sales (2.55 kg/day loose) were 40% below the 45-day average used (4.3 kg/day). The 45 days held
  two peaks (restock 28 Sep, Fan Favourites + Halloween 26 Oct). Always compare and report.

## Stock-gap lesson

The owner first believed about 90 kg had gone missing (start "92 + 540 kg" vs the count). Using the Inventory tab's
own "Ordered Quantity" (842.8 kg) minus the count (247.8 kg) = 595 kg out, against website 557 kg + PR 22 + atypical
21.5 + sampling 20 + friends 4 = about 624 kg: no gap. Starting figures from memory are unreliable; reconcile from
the sheets.
