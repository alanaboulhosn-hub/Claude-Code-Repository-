Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Where do you deliver?
We deliver all over Lebanon except for Nabatieh and bordering cities.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery is based on when the order reaches the warehouse. Upon arriving, delivery in Beirut is usually within 1–2 business days, and outside Beirut in 2–3 business days. We don’t deliver on Sundays.
What form of payment do you have?
We offer cash on delivery in all areas. Will be introducing Whish shortly.
Is it gelatin-free / halal?
Yes, the majority of our sweets are gelatin-free/halal, with the exception of some, which are labeled.
How can I contact you?
You can reach us by WhatsApp 79411565, email hello@swedishfikalb.com and on Instagram @swedishfika.lb. We are always happy to answer your questions.
Do you have a shop I can visit?
We’re online-only for now, and we deliver across Lebanon.
Wrong order / Missing item?
We’re sorry about that. Please message us with your order details and we’ll make it right.
